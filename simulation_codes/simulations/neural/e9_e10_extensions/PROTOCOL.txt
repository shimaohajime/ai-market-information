E9: continue all9 E6 both-group endpoints from update6 through12, with unchanged
learning budgets and fresh round-indexed data. At adoption.5 for all3 seeds, also
run6 updates with COMMON primitive simulation innovations and optimizer order at
every update. Prices/histories are recomputed after every policy change; this is
not frozen training data or a population learning map. Four independent one-step
refits reset to each FRESH update12 adoption.5 profile estimate conditional update
noise and mean drift. These are4 refits per3 markets, not12 independent markets.
Metrics: J,A,delta and low/high/other iota. J is analytical A^2/supply variance.
Operational stability: maximum relative changes in J and all3 iotas below0.5%
for the final3 updates; late3-state vs preceding3-state J means within1%.
Fixed-law drift check: each metric's mean percent change +/-t(3)*SE entirely in
[-1%,1%]. A failure does not prove economic instability; a pass is not equilibrium.
Period2 screen on each7-state extension: at least4 sign reversals among6 J steps,
mean lag2 state-return distance / mean lag1 state-change distance <.25, and median
lag1 distance >.5%. Distances are Euclidean in [J,iotaL,iotaH,iotaOther], scaled by
update6 levels. Report the continuous diagnostics and noise scale, not merely flag.
This short experiment cannot rule out longer cycles or prove population convergence.

E10: matched control plus2 changes to dividends. All other primitives remain fixed.
Private signals remain theta+noise. Let psi be a unit-variance Gaussian latent
factor orthogonal to the original latent target under the stationary covariance.
Control: payoff mean=lambda*theta, noise variance=.43^2.
Nonaligned: mean=lambda*(sqrt(.8)*theta+sqrt(.2*prior_variance)*psi). Thus20% of the
fundamental dividend variance comes from a factor absent from the private signal;
unconditional fundamental variance is matched to control. Not a private-information
precision measure for the WHOLE dividend: J still measures fresh information on theta.
Heteroskedastic: mean=lambda*theta; noise variance=.43^2*(1+.6*tanh(public0/SDpublic0)).
Variance multiplier in(.4,1.6), unconditional mean1 by symmetry. Current variance is
a function of released public data; current standard-normal payoff noise independent.
The mean/variance forecasting head remains Model A. Constant fitted variance is
intentionally misspecified in the heteroskedastic economy. This is NOT Model B.
