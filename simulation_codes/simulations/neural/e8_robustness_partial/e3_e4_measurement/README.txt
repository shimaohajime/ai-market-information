E3 price-sharing prediction measurements and E4 private capacity value
29 October 2026

Start with PROTOCOL.txt and deliverables/E3_E4_Measurement_20261029.pdf.
Run in Python3.12 with requirements.txt installed and CPU PyTorch2.5.1.
  export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1
  export OMP_NUM_THREADS=1
  python -m unittest discover -s . -p 'test_*.py' -v
  python run_measurement.py --workers 7
  python analyze.py
  python make_report.py
Completed market jobs are resumed. For complete fresh regeneration, move results/
elsewhere and create empty results/ and data/ directories. Intermediate training
panels are regenerated by seed keys and removed after use; output retains candidate
models, immutable market models, initial baseline models, calibrated heads, training
curves, independent path scores and numerical diagnostics.

Primary comparisons:
  summary/seed_results.csv: per-seed E3 gains and E4 upgrades.
  summary/intervals.csv: paired 3-seed means and descriptive t(df2) intervals,
  for levels and changes from adoption0. Uncertainty is limited with only3 seeds.
  summary/CE_diagnostics.csv: exponential-weight concentration and half comparisons.
  summary/CE_evaluation_uncertainty.csv: paired, bank-stratified path bootstrap1000
  draws, conditional on trained models, not an additional model-training replication.
  summary/VALIDATION.json and TEST_RESULTS.txt: market integrity and measurement checks.

No neural equilibrium certification, no nonlinear mutual-information measurement,
no aggregate welfare, no net value after computing cost, no population-optimal
capacity ranking. The nested(zL,zH) observer/candidate uses BOTH encoders, hence
more compute than a stand-alone width32 candidate. Its final head is fitted on a
separate calibration bank and is labelled separately from the primary neural head.
The E6 market-generation code and primitives are copied unchanged in engine.py and
reference_capacity/. Immutable source models are included in markets/ and baselines/.
