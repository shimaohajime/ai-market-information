Finite neural retraining implementation

The market has eight public coordinates with balanced target loadings and noisy
cubic releases. The fundamental follows theta = sqrt(0.8) theta_old +
sqrt(0.2) eta, and the payout is D = 1.15 theta + epsilon with SD(epsilon)=0.43.
Private-noise variances are (0.25, 0.09), investor masses are (0.55, 0.45),
risk aversions are (0.9, 1.2), and supply-noise SD is 0.9.

Adoption x is the high-capacity fraction within group 1. Group 1 uses low- and
high-capacity GRUs of widths 4 and 32, a 16-period public-information window,
and constant-affine Gaussian heads. Group 2 uses its private clue and the
current price, with affine coefficients and residual variance refitted under
each market law. The zero-mass capacity cell is fitted as a counterfactual
candidate.

For every x in {0, 0.5, 1}, start from the same inherited profile and simulate
the frozen-policy market. Then follow six simultaneous retraining updates. At
each update, freeze deployed policies, generate all training and evaluation data
from the recursively clearing market, fit permitted policies only after data
generation is complete, and deploy all newly fitted policies together.

The training bank contains 512 paths by 2,048 usable observations after a
512-date burn-in (1,048,576 observations). Validation and independent
head-calibration banks each contain 65,536 observations; evaluation contains
131,072. No windows cross path boundaries. Both GRUs are warm-started and
refitted using eight chunks, Adam learning rate 0.001 for five chunks decaying
to 0.00001 over three, batch size 256, weight penalty 1e-5, and gradient-norm
clip 3. The group-2 head is refitted by OLS. Optimizer state resets each update;
normalization remains fixed.

Primitive streams are indexed by seed, update, and bank. Adoption shares share
primitive shocks but use recursively recomputed prices. Evaluation streams do
not enter optimization or selection. Report beta, v, iota, A, J=A^2/0.9^2,
price moments, held-out MSE, log score, variance calibration, and independent
full/no-private observer comparisons. J is a structural fresh-price precision
measure, not mutual information or total price informativeness.

The calculation is a finite-sample, finite-optimization approximation to the
learning map. Coupled-initial-price and path-moment diagnostics do not establish
global stability, uniqueness, or a population retraining equilibrium. Unit
tests verify clearing, analytically solvable constant-policy markets, causal
timing, inactive-capacity isolation, path boundaries, and frozen-model
fingerprints.
